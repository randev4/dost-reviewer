  - **B:** 10
  - **C:** 12
  - **D:** 16
- **Answer Key:** **C** (12)

**Explanation:**
The first fold (left-to-right) creates a mirror image, doubling the holes. The second fold (bottom-to-top) again doubles the holes, resulting in 4 layers. Thus, the total number of holes is $8 + 4 = 12$.

## dost1001-0098

- **Topic:** spatial | **Difficulty Level:** 3
- **Question:** A strip of four square panels labeled 1-2-3-4 is wrapped around the side faces of a cube so that panel 1 is front, panel 2 is right, panel 3 is back, and panel 4 is left; the top and bottom are unmarked. The cube is then rolled once to the right (top moves to right) and once forward (top moves to front). Which pair is on the TOP and FRONT at the end?
- **Image:** `dost1001-q0098.png`
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** Top 1, Front 2
  - **B:** Top 2, Front 3
  - **C:** Top 3, Front 4
  - **D:** Top 4, Front 1
- **Answer Key:** **C** (Top 3, Front 4)

**Explanation:**
Initially, Panel 1 is Front, Panel 2 is Right, Panel 3 is Back, and Panel 4 is Left. When the cube rolls once to the right, Panel 1 moves to the Left, Panel 2 moves to the Front, Panel 3 moves to the Right, and Panel 4 moves to the Back; the original Right face (Panel 2) becomes the new Front, and the original Top face becomes the new Right face. When it then rolls once forward, the new Front face (Panel 2) moves to the Bottom, the new Top face moves to the Front, and the new Back face (Panel 4) moves to the Top. Therefore, Panel 3 (which was initially Back, then Right after the first roll) will be on the Top, and Panel 4 (which was initially Left, then Back after the first roll) will be on the Front.

- **Question:** The 8-cell board shown must be reduced to the target 5-cell shape by removing one connected three-cell piece. One cell of the removable region contains a small directional mark. Which loose piece matches both the region's shape and the mark, allowing rotation but not reflection?
- **Image:** `dost1001-q0099.png`
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** Piece A
  - **B:** Piece B
  - **C:** Piece C
  - **D:** Piece D
- **Answer Key:** **D** (Piece D)

**Explanation:**
The target 5-cell shape is formed by removing a 3-cell L-shaped piece from the 8-cell board. The removable region is the L-shaped piece consisting of the top-left cell, the cell below it, and the cell to its right, with the directional mark in the top-left cell pointing right. Only Piece D matches this L-shape and the directional mark after a $90^\circ$ clockwise rotation.

## dost1001-0100

- **Topic:** spatial | **Difficulty Level:** 3
- **Question:** Which option completes the final cell of the $3\times3$ spatial matrix?
- **Image:** `dost1001-q0100.png`
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** Option A
  - **B:** Option B
  - **C:** Option C
  - **D:** Option D
- **Answer Key:** **A** (Option A)

**Explanation:**
In each row, the first figure is rotated $90^\circ$ clockwise to get the second figure, and then the second figure is rotated $90^\circ$ clockwise to get the third figure. Applying this rule to the third row, the second figure (a square with a diagonal line from top-left to bottom-right) is rotated $90^\circ$ clockwise to become a square with a diagonal line from top-right to bottom-left, which corresponds to Option A.
