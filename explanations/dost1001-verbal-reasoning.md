  - **D:** The indicator can flash only when the alarm sounds.

The statement "Whenever the alarm sounds, the red indicator flashes" establishes a conditional relationship where the alarm sounding is a sufficient condition for the indicator to flash. ## dost1001-0140

- **Topic:** verbal-logic | **Difficulty Level:** 3
- **Question:** If a specimen is metallic, it is electrically conductive. Specimen Q is not electrically conductive. Which conclusion follows?
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** Q is metallic.
  - **B:** Q is an insulator in every condition.
  - **C:** Q is magnetic.
  - **D:** Q is not metallic.
- **Answer Key:** **D** (Q is not metallic.)

**Explanation:**
The statement "If a specimen is metallic, it is electrically conductive" can be written as $P \rightarrow Q$, where $P$ is "a specimen is metallic" and $Q$ is "it is electrically conductive." We are given that "Specimen Q is not electrically conductive," which is $\neg Q$. By the rule of contrapositive (or modus tollens), if $P \rightarrow Q$ is true and $\neg Q$ is true, then $\neg P$ must also be true. Therefore, Specimen Q is not metallic.

- **Question:** Complete the relationship matrix: ROW 1: BOOK $\to$ READ $\to$ KNOWLEDGE; ROW 2: FOOD $\to$ EAT $\to$ NOURISHMENT; ROW 3: MAP $\to$ CONSULT $\to$ ___.
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** distance
  - **B:** paper
  - **C:** travel
  - **D:** direction
- **Answer Key:** **D** (direction)

**Explanation:**
The relationship in each row follows the pattern: Object $\to$ Action $\to$ Outcome/Purpose. You READ a BOOK to gain KNOWLEDGE. You EAT FOOD to get NOURISHMENT. Similarly, you CONSULT a MAP to find DIRECTION.

## dost1001-0148

- **Topic:** verbal-logic | **Difficulty Level:** 3
- **Question:** Complete the matrix by applying the same relation in each row: ROW 1: SCARCE $\to$ RARE $\to$ ABUNDANT; ROW 2: TIMID $\to$ SHY $\to$ BOLD; ROW 3: FRAGILE $\to$ DELICATE $\to$ ___.
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** durable
  - **B:** breakable
  - **C:** light
  - **D:** careful
- **Answer Key:** **A** (durable)

**Explanation:**
The relationship in each row is a synonym, followed by an antonym of the first two words. SCARCE is a synonym for RARE, and ABUNDANT is an antonym. TIMID is a synonym for SHY, and BOLD is an antonym. Therefore, FRAGILE is a synonym for DELICATE, and the antonym for fragile/delicate is DURABLE.

## dost1001-0149

- **Topic:** verbal-logic | **Difficulty Level:** 3
- **Question:** Complete the matrix: ROW 1: ORE $\to$ SMELT $\to$ METAL; ROW 2: GRAIN $\to$ MILL $\to$ FLOUR; ROW 3: MILK $\to$ ___ $\to$ BUTTER.
- **Choices:**
  - **A:** freeze
  - **B:** boil
  - **C:** churn
  - **D:** ferment
- **Answer Key:** **C** (churn)

**Explanation:**
The matrix illustrates a three-step process where a raw material undergoes a specific transformation to yield a final product. For milk to become butter, it must undergo the process of churning, which separates the butterfat from the buttermilk.

- **Choices:**

  - **C:** patient

- **Answer Key:** **B** (diagnosis)

The matrix establishes a logical progression from an initial state, through an intermediate analytical step, to a final outcome. In the context of symptoms, observation leads to a diagnosis, which is the identification of the nature of an illness or other problem.
